# Coach Companion Passport

create a user interface (UI/UX) for Coach Companion Passport (CCP) done for the lebanese basketball federation (logo attached), landing page + mock admin login, when the admin logs in he should have a system to create/edit/delete coaches and see the coaches data bases, those coaches should be filtered and searched by their names, phone numbers & levels (level 1, level 2 ....) for each coach we will have a profile with his ID information, his CV & a CPD points tracker and how much left to be eligible for next level, and his flb certifications (downloadable), the workshops & clinics attended and how much CPD points each added to his score, his assessments grades & results  and a  photo gallery filtered by event. There will be also a portal for each coach to register himself on the system do a face verification (KYC video look right left up and down) for registration, then can access his profile to upload his CV and download his Certifications and view his data updated & his CPD, he can also download an E-card in PDF that you will design as well (very professional & visually simple but attractive an advanced) with a QR code specific and generated for him on his downloaded card (passport), once scanned this QR will display his information with CPD & essential results for the person who scanned . Build it all within the 5 credits score, this is a mock app as a design only , no real functionalities, just the flow and the design
